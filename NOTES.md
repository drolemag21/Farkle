## How the code is organized
- Source: `farkle-table.html`. Classic rules, 2-4 players online. (It began next to Farkle Showdown, a separate single-player roguelite that lives outside this repo and shares no code.)
- Host-authoritative: the room creator's page (`S.M`) is the only writer. Players send intents (`act` → `hostIntent`: sync, start, again, sel, keep, roll, bank); the host answers with an event (`hostEmit`: GAME_STARTED, DICE_SET_ASIDE, DICE_KEPT, DICE_ROLLED, FARKLE, BANKED, TURN_PASSED, GAME_OVER, LOBBY, PLAYER_LEFT/BACK, HOST_CHANGED) plus the full snapshot. Clients only render snapshots (`applyHost`, which validates them with `cleanState`) and play effects for events (`playEvent`).
- Dice: the host picks values with `crypto` and a seed; `makeLaunch(seed)` gives launch positions/velocities, sent in `st.roll`. Every screen runs `simulate(L)` (fixed 120 Hz step, only + - * / sqrt, so identical everywhere) and plays it back; the tumble angle is tied to the path still to travel, so each die lands on its value. The felt is `W` 300 × `H` 340, taller than wide so the dice are big on a phone; throw strength is one constant, `THROW` (scales launch speed, spin and, more gently, height in `makeLaunch`). 1 was the original soft toss (dice settle mid-table, rarely touch a rail); it's now 2.2 at the owner's request: dice hit the far rail about 1.5 times each and rebound across the felt, settling in ~1.45 s median, 2.0 s worst over 2,000 seeds (cap `MAX_STEPS` is 6 s). Changing `W`, `H` or the launch numbers changes every roll, so players on an old and a new copy would see different dice: ship such changes as an "everyone reload" release.
- Table view and layout: the camera (`CAM`) sits high over the near rail looking down at about 75 degrees, and `BOUNDS` frames the felt plus a sliver of rail (the outer rails run off the sides). `resize` gives the canvas the screen height left under the score cards after the turn panel (`#turn`) and the footer, capped at the full-width size; when keeping the footer would leave the table under 320 px (iPhone SE size), the footer sits just below the fold instead. A ResizeObserver on the board, table, turn panel and footer plus window `resize` refit it. Score cards are one line (dot, name, score, a hairline progress bar); four across (480 px+) they stack. Card status shows only "Away" now: the glow marks the roller. The footer shows the latest action (`st.log`), or the final-round warning, plus Leave; the target is printed on the felt.
- Transports share one interface (`join/publish/sendIntent/setMe/leave`): `RoomNet` (Claude room; everything rides on presence because event topics need Contributor access; 4 KiB presence cap), `SupaNet` (Supabase Realtime Broadcast + Presence on public channels, supabase-js 2.118.0 loaded from jsDelivr on demand; only works when the file is hosted outside Claude, since Artifacts block outside connections), `TabsNet` (BroadcastChannel, for testing in two tabs). Settings → Network picks one; Automatic uses the room inside Claude and Supabase elsewhere.
- Hosted copy: `node tools/build-site.js` writes `docs/index.html` (GitHub Pages, served from `/docs` on `main`) from `farkle-table.html` plus `site.config.json`. It adds the doctype/charset/viewport head and the `[hidden]` rule (several panels set `display: flex` and would ignore `hidden` without it), and sets `window.FARKLE_HOSTED = { url, key }`; `CFG` uses those when the player hasn't saved their own, so players never see Settings. Uses the Supabase **publishable** key (`sb_publishable_...`; legacy anon keys retire in 2027). Settings → Save rejects `sb_secret_` keys.
- Presence: a player missing for `GRACE` (3.5 s) is marked away and their turns are skipped (counted in `skips`). If the host goes missing, the first connected seat takes over (`hostCheck` → `becomeHost`); ties between two hosts go to the snapshot that is `ahead`. A reloaded tab rejoins its room and seat from sessionStorage (`fk.sess`, `fk.pid`), and a reloaded host restores `fk.host`.
- Sound (`SND`, all Web Audio synthesis, no files): starts on the first tap or key (`unlockAudio`, listening to pointerdown, pointerup, touchend, click and keydown: on touch screens pointerdown is not a user gesture, so phones need the others). `unlock` sets `navigator.audioSession.type = "playback"` where supported so the iOS silent switch doesn't mute it, and resumes an "interrupted" context too. Lounge bus = jazz trio in F at 74 bpm (`PROG`, `scheduleBar`: FM Rhodes with tremolo, walking bass, brushes, ride, sparse vibes) + sparse room sounds (chip clacks, glass clinks, a distant slot jingle), queued 1.5 s ahead by `pump`. The old brown-noise crowd murmur was removed (owner: it sounded like wind and sat over the music). `syncSound` sets the lounge level: 1 outside play (start screen, lobby, game over); during play 0 (fades out) unless "Music during games" is on (`#gmusic` header button, shown only on the game screen, localStorage `fk.gmusic`), then 0.6. Table sounds: `roll(sim, delay)` plays a `whoosh` from release to the first felt landing, then the impacts the physics step records in `sim.hits` (felt thud, rail knock, die-on-die `clack`) plus a slide hiss following dice speed. Die-on-die hits before the first felt landing are skipped: the handful starts packed and grazes in the air, and those clicks read as a keep. `clack` is a dry unpitched noise hit, deliberately unlike the pitched `click` that `tray` uses, for the same reason (owner); `select`, `tray` (die set down in the tray), `bank` (chip stack + bell), `hot` (whoosh, thump, crackle, vibes run), `applause(big)` (16 synthesized pairs of hands in the hall reverb; on BANKED when the turn had hot dice (`M.th`, sent as `d.hot`) or banked 1,000+, longer when both), `farkle` (thump, then the trio hits one dark chord on the table bus: D half-diminished on the Rhodes, low D on the bass, a choked cymbal; `rhodes` and `bassNote` take an optional bus for this, scaled by `FARKLE_BAND`. Owner picked it from mockups over a tape stop, a bass slide and a choked bell; it replaced a muted "sad trombone" horn, too corny, and a rake + mallet version, too flat), `turn` (two vibes notes, on TURN_PASSED / GAME_STARTED when it becomes your turn). Mute button in the header, kept in localStorage (`fk.mute`). Levels checked with `SND.measure(part)` (offline render, 10 s window): music about -26 dBFS RMS; hot -38, farkle -37, applause -41, bank -44, turn -44.
- Dice colours: `DICE_COLORS` (red, green (lime-leaning, to stand off the felt), blue, orange, purple, cyan). Picked on the start screen and in the lobby (`renderDicePickers`, `setDiceColor`), kept in localStorage (`fk.dice`), sent in presence as `me.dc`, copied to `player.dc` by the host in the lobby. The renderer paints all dice in the current roller's colour (`BODY`, set per frame in `draw`). The lobby slot shows a small swatch.
- Leader crown: `renderGame` puts a crown (`crownEl`) on the card of a single outright leader (none at 0 or in a tie); it pops in only when the leader changes (`S.leader`).
- Names are capped at 12 characters (`NAME_MAX`): `maxlength` on the field, `enterRoom` refuses a longer name (e.g. one saved in `fk.name` before the cap) with a toast, and `cleanName` cuts names from other browsers to 12. Hint under the field: "12 characters max." with a live count (`renderNameHint`). Measured at 375 px: about 12 typical characters fit beside " (you)"; wide letters fit fewer. Score cards are one line now (name beside the score): re-measure if you change their font sizes. On wide screens (4-column board, 480 px+) cards are narrower and names cut sooner.
- Turn flow (owner's design, from player feedback asking for a set-aside step): turn stage `ts` goes pre → rolled → kept → rolled → ... → banked (or farkle). After a roll the only button is a gold **Keep** (`#btn-roll` relabelled; sends `keep`): tapped dice stay tentative (`sel`, `s: 1`) until Keep, which scores them together (`hostKeep`, one keep per roll, so a triple can't be split into singles) and locks them (`s: 2`). After a keep: gold **Roll N** (or "Hot dice: roll 6") and **Bank** (`hostBank` only from `kept`, so the banked total is already final). The gold button is always the next step; with nothing picked, Keep is greyed out because the next step is tapping dice. Keys: 1-6 pick, R or K press the gold button, B banks.
- Keep glide: on DICE_KEPT the dice just kept lift off the felt and arc into the tray one after another (`glideToTray`, `S.glide`, poses `where: "glide"`, drawn above the near rail). `applyHost` takes the pre-snapshot `poses()` as the glide's start positions. The glide happens at Keep, so the next throw no longer waits for it (the `wait` path on DICE_ROLLED is left in place but is now 0). Timing: `GLIDE_MS` 380 per die, `GLIDE_STAGGER` 45, `GLIDE_GAP` 90.
- Hot dice flair (`hotFlair`, `drawFx`): embers rising off the felt and a heat glow on the canvas, dice tumble with ember-orange edges (`anim.hot`), neon "Hot dice!" banner that sputters on, a short scorched shake (`.scorch`) and the roller's chip glowing (`data-hot`).
- Local testing: see `CLAUDE.md` → Testing. `tests/serve.js` is a tiny static server; it wraps skeleton-less `.html` files in the same head the Artifact publisher adds, so the source behaves as it does on claude.ai.
