Fixing the mode picker

Why people pick wrong today

  • The first choice is the wrong one. Classic / Custom sits first and looks like "game mode", but it's only the rules. The real choice (online, join, or bots) comes after.
  • Three actions, no explanation. Create room, Play vs bots and Join a friend sit stacked with equal weight and no words about what each one does.
  • "Play vs 2 bots" is the only button that sounds like playing. "Create room" sounds like setup, so new players skip it and land in a solo game.
  • Rules apply to two paths but not the third. They're shown even when you're about to join someone else's room.

What the redesign has to do

  • Ask who you're playing with before anything else.
  • Say in plain words what happens after each tap (you get a code, you need a code, it starts now on this phone).
  • Show rules only where they matter, and as one line, not two big cards.
  • Fit on a 375 px phone with no scrolling for the choice itself.

My recommendation: B

The problem is that people act before choosing. B is the only option that makes the choice the whole screen, and the sheets give each path a sentence about what comes next. The extra tap costs less than a confused first game. C is a fine stopgap if you want something today, but it won't stop skimmers.

Also worth doing, whichever you pick

  • After a bots game ends, offer "Play this with friends" next to Play again. It catches the people who picked bots by mistake.
  • Remember the last path on this device and open its sheet one tap faster next time.
  • Rename "Custom" to a "Change" link on the rules line. Two big rule cards on the home screen are what read as modes.
